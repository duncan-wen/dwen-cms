# Series: an approved list, ordered by date, apart from Tags and URLs

The blog-v1 spec left Series out, but Posts now arrive that are meant to be read in order (the Python and then the Go concurrency Posts), and Readers can't see that. A Series is an id in an approved list (`content/series.yml`) that a Post names in front matter; it is not a kind of Tag, because Tags have no order and a Post carries several, so neither 「第幾篇」 nor 「下一篇」 could be answered from them. A Post's place in its Series follows its date rather than a `part` number, since a later Post in a Series is always published later and a number would be one more field to keep right. URLs stay `/posts/<slug>`, so joining or leaving a Series never touches Slug history or redirects. A Series is derived from its Published Posts and shown only once it has 2 of them, so it never has a lifecycle of its own: no Draft, no Takedown, just a 404 when it drops below 2. The cost: a Post can't be slotted in before an older one without adding part numbers, and a Post belongs to at most one Series.
